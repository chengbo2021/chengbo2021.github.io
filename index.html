About Bo Cheng

Better agriculture needs both scientific rigor and a clear view of the world beyond the field.

Bo Cheng works at the intersection of crop science, agricultural systems modeling, and sustainability. His interests center on using quantitative evidence to understand agricultural choices and support better outcomes for producers, landscapes, and communities.

This platform is a starting point for research exchange, interdisciplinary collaboration, and practical ideas that can be tested in the real world.
