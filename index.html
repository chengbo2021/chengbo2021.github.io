What I help with

Go global with evidence,
not guesswork.

Many agricultural products are highly competitive in their home markets. In a new country, success depends on local climate, soils, farming practices, regulations, and trust. I help companies answer these questions early—before major investment.

About Bo Cheng
“

Going global isn't just exporting products—it's earning trust in someone else's fields.

Bo Cheng works at the intersection of crop science, agricultural systems modeling, and international agriculture. Working across languages and cultures, he helps agricultural companies understand growing conditions in new markets, generate credible local evidence, and build lasting partnerships across borders.

The goal is simple: help good agricultural technologies reach the farmers who need them, wherever they are—responsibly and sustainably.
