Bo Cheng is a crop modeling scientist and PhD candidate in Crop, Soil, and Environmental Sciences at Auburn University, a leading U.S. land-grant university. He builds and applies process-based DSSAT crop models to predict yield, greenhouse gas emissions, and soil-amendment effects—and grounds them in multi-season field experiments. His research has appeared in leading journals including Global Change Biology and Journal of Cleaner Production, and he contributes to an NSF-funded effort on engineered biochar. With research and leadership experience across continents, he turns rigorous science into decisions businesses can act on—in any market.
